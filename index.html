What Is Voice SMS Service & Voice SMS Service Provider?

A Voice SMS Service is a modern telephony communication method where automated pre-recorded voice clips or automated alerts are transmitted to a mobile or landline number instead of standard text messages. While text pranks can be conducted in our dedicated SMS Bomber, Voice SMS is widely used commercially for:

  • Automated appointment & event reminders
  • Critical emergency community broadcasts
  • Urgent two-factor authentication (OTP) verification calls
  • Billing and transaction update alerts
  • Customer support callbacks and interactive voice response (IVR) surveys

A Voice SMS Service Provider supplies the underlying cloud telephony infrastructure, Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) gateways, and carrier interconnects needed to transmit voice payloads at scale. Providers ensure high call completion rates, multi-lingual audio synthesis, and automated call retry queues.

How to Protect Yourself from Call Bombers?

If you or someone you know is receiving frequent automated calls, you can immediately regain control and protect your peace of mind by following these effective measures:

  • Activate Do Not Disturb (DND): Register your number on the National DND registry (e.g. by sending START 0 to 1909 in India) to block unsolicited commercial and automated calls.
  • Enable Built-in Spam Filters: Both Android (Google Phone) and iPhone (iOS) include native options to screen suspected spam calls and silence unknown callers automatically. Read our complete guide on how to block spam calls on Android and iPhone.
  • Install Verified Caller Identification Apps: Applications like Truecaller or Hiya help flag high-volume automated numbers in real time before your phone even rings.
  • Temporarily Mute Unknown Callers: If you are under an active prank wave, turning on Do Not Disturb for 15–30 minutes allows the automated wave queues to expire without disturbing you.
  • Report Persistent Harassment: Contact your cellular provider or local cybercrime desk if an unknown caller persists after being warned.
