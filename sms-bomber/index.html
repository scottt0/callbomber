How to Prevent SMS Bombing Attacks

If you are on the receiving end of an intense SMS flood, you can stop it quickly:

  • Enable Spam Protection: Turn on spam filtering in Google Messages or Apple iMessage to automatically mute unknown message floods.
  • Register for DND: Activate Do Not Disturb with your mobile network provider to block commercial SMS streams.
  • Mute Notifications Briefly: Putting your phone on Do Not Disturb for 15 minutes allows automated queues to complete quietly.

What is a Bulk SMS Sender & Bulk SMS Gateway?

A Bulk SMS Sender is an enterprise platform used by organizations to transmit notices, reminders, and OTPs to thousands of customers simultaneously. A Bulk SMS Gateway is the technical telecom interface that routes these SMS packets directly into mobile carrier infrastructure with real-time delivery reporting.

Best Practices for SMS Security

Always keep your phone number confidential on public online forums, enable two-factor authentication via authenticator apps (rather than SMS alone), and avoid clicking unverified links inside unexpected text messages.
