How to Block Spam Calls on Android & iPhone

By Call Bomber Security Lab • 4 min read • Telecom & Device Protection

Unwanted automated robocalls, unsolicited telemarketers, and prank dialer attempts can be an annoying disruption. Modern smartphones include powerful native tools to screen, filter, and silence suspected spam automatically without needing expensive third-party apps.

1. How to Block Spam Calls on Android (Google Pixel, Motorola, Xiaomi)

Stock Android and Google Phone apps feature built-in machine-learning spam detection:

  1. Open the standard Phone app on your device.
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the upper right corner and select Settings.
  3. Tap on Caller ID & Spam.
  4. Turn on See caller and spam ID.
  5. Enable Filter spam calls. When enabled, your phone will prevent suspected spam calls from ringing altogether.

2. How to Block Spam Calls on Samsung Galaxy Phones

Samsung smartphones integrate Hiya caller intelligence directly into One UI:

  1. Open the Phone app.
  2. Tap More options (⋮) > Settings.
  3. Tap on Caller ID and spam protection and toggle it ON.
  4. Select Block spam and scam calls and choose either Block all spam and scam calls or Only block high-risk scam calls.

3. How to Silence Spam & Unknown Callers on iPhone (iOS)

Apple includes a dedicated switch in iOS to eliminate unwanted ringings:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Scroll down and tap Phone.
  3. Tap Silence Unknown Callers and toggle the switch to ON.
  4. Calls from numbers not in your contacts or recent outbound calls will be silently directed to voicemail without ringing your device.

4. Activating National Carrier DND (Do Not Disturb)

In countries like India, the national telecom regulator (TRAI) enforces strict Do Not Disturb (DND) registries that prohibit telemarketers and commercial auto-dialers from contacting registered subscribers.

Quick Tip: Send an SMS with the text START 0 to 1909 from your mobile connection to register your number for full National Do Not Disturb (DND) across all commercial categories.

Summary: Proactive Steps for Peace of Mind

By combining native phone spam filters, carrier DND registries, and exercising caution when sharing numbers on public social media forums, you can keep your mobile device completely quiet and protected against spam dialers.
